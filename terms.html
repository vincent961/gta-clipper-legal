1.What this app is

GTA Clipper is a personal desktop tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and posts short video clips to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Snapchat, using the official APIs of those platforms.

2.Who may use it

The app is for the owner's personal use only. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up and no other users. If you authorize the app with your account, you do so only to post to your own account.

3.Content

The app posts the owner's own gameplay clips. Clips from other creators' livestreams are only posted with the creator's permission or as part of their official clipping program; they are always credited by name with a link to their channel and are removed on request (see Contact). The account owner chooses what is posted. The account owner is responsible for what is posted and for following the rules of each platform, including the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4.Platform rules

Use of each platform through this app is also subject to that platform's own terms. This app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (see also the Google Privacy Policy). The app does not try to get around any limits, reviews or restrictions of those platforms.

5.No warranty

The app is provided as is, without any warranty. It may stop working when a platform changes its API.
