GTA6 Clip Uploader

A personal desktop tool that cuts short GTA clips from my own gameplay recordings and from public livestreams (always credited to the streamer), and posts them to my own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat) through their official APIs.

Uploads to YouTube use YouTube API Services (YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy).

It is not a public service: only the owner uses it, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own accounts.
