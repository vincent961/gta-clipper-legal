GTA Clipper

A personal desktop tool that cuts short clips from my own GTA gameplay recordings and posts them to my own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat) through their official APIs.

It is not a public service: only the owner uses it, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own accounts.

Clips from other creators' livestreams are only posted with the creator's permission or as part of their official clipping program, and are always credited.
