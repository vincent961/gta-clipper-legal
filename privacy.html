Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

GTA6 Clip Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.

2. YouTube API Services

GTA6 Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, to read view counts of those videos, and to look up which hashtags are used on popular public GTA videos. By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What the app stores

  • Access tokens that the platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat) give after the owner logs in. They are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer and are used only to post the owner's videos and to read the view counts of those videos.
  • Basic profile info of the connected account (such as the username), only to show which account is connected.
  • Video statistics (view counts of the owner's own posts), stored locally so the owner can see which posts did well.
  • Hashtag list built from public YouTube search results (titles and descriptions of popular GTA videos). Only the hashtags are kept; the list is replaced every day and never kept longer than 30 days. No data about the people who made those videos is stored.

4. What the app does not do

  • It has no server and no database; nothing is sent to anyone other than the platform being posted to.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking.
  • It does not use data for advertising and does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not access private data of other users.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect at any time by deleting the local token file, and by removing the app's access in the settings of each platform:

  • YouTube / Google: Google security settings, third-party access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings, Apps and websites.
  • Snapchat: Settings, Connected apps.

After access is revoked, the owner deletes the stored tokens and the statistics read through that platform within 7 days, or right away on request.

6. Clips of other creators

Some clips come from public livestreams of other creators. They are always credited by name with a link to the creator's channel. Creators who want a clip removed can contact us and it will be taken down.
