Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Summary

GTA6 Clip Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.

2. What the app stores

  • Access tokens that the platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat) give after the owner logs in. They are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer and are used only to post the owner's own videos and to read the view counts of those videos.
  • Basic profile info of the connected account (such as the username), only to show which account is connected.
  • Video statistics (view counts of the owner's own posts), stored locally to see which posts did well.

3. What the app does not do

  • It has no server and no database; nothing is sent to anyone other than the platform being posted to.
  • It does not use data for advertising and does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not access data of other users.

4. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect at any time by deleting the local token file, and by removing the app's access in the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions).

5. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.

6. Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/vincent961/gta-clipper-legal/issues.
